# Metric Framework

## Core Idea

The project uses registry-visible omissions as proxies for what is being hidden, blurred, or made hard to inspect.

This is not a legal adjudication. A study can be missing information for benign reasons, legacy schema reasons, or because the field is not applicable. The value of the analysis is comparative: it shows where omission concentrates by sponsor class, study type, phase, and lead sponsor.

## Hiddenness Domains

### Structural omission

- missing official title
- missing detailed description
- missing location records
- missing primary outcome entries
- primary outcomes present but without descriptions
- missing IPD sharing statement
- no linked publication references
- missing phase on interventional studies
- missing arm groups on interventional studies

### Closure-stage non-disclosure

- closed study without an actual primary completion date
- closed study without an actual full completion date
- closed study without actual enrollment
- terminated, withdrawn, or suspended study without `whyStopped`
- interventional study with an actual primary completion date at least 2 years old and still no posted results

## Why A 2-Year Results Gap

This project reports both 1-year and 2-year no-results gaps, but uses the 2-year gap in the composite score. That makes the signal more conservative and reduces the risk of treating borderline cases as clear non-disclosure.

## Composite Score

The `hiddenness_score` is a simple count of omission flags. It is not intended as a regulatory scorecard. It is a compact way to compare sponsor classes and large sponsor organizations on overall information loss.

## Interpretation

- High absolute counts show where the largest volume of obscured information sits.
- High rates show where omission is systematically concentrated.
- Industry may dominate counts because it registers many studies.
- Other classes may dominate rates because their records are structurally sparse.

Both views matter and are reported separately.

## Reproducible results-gap denominator (corrected 18 October 2026)

Previous calculations allowed studies with **estimated or partially specified primary-completion dates** into the age window, and derived the rate denominator from elapsed time rather than the same eligibility criteria used for the numerator. This could give incorrect rates, particularly in all-study and sponsor-class summaries.

The corrected `eligible_results_gap_1y` and `eligible_results_gap_2y` flags require **all** the following for a registry-observed gap analysis: the trial is interventional, the registry status belongs to the project's defined closed group, the primary completion date is marked `ACTUAL` **and** is precisely `YYYY-MM-DD`, the API `hasResults` field is a genuine boolean without a conflicting results-post date, and the one-/two-calendar-year anniversary has elapsed as of the **source dataset date**. The numerator is the subset of that same population with `hasResults=false`. A February 29 completion is screened using March 1 for non-leap anniversaries. This is a screening convention, **not** a statutory deadline.

The output retains the source `primary_completion_date` string and adds `primary_completion_date_precision`, `results_first_post_date_precision`, `results_status_known`, `results_status_conflict` and both eligibility flags. Day-based lags are only calculated when the source dates are exact; a partial `YYYY-MM` is **not** silently treated as the first of the month. `eligible_results_gap_2y_count` is now an actual count of eligible cohort rows rather than an unrelated date-only count.

The analysis uses the API's `api_data_timestamp` (falling back to `fetched_at` for legacy downloads), rather than the wall clock, for `as-of` calculations. It rejects an explicit different `--as-of-date` because present-day posted-results status does not establish what was posted on an earlier/later day. A filtered source query is excluded from full-registry interpretation unless `--allow-query-subset` is explicitly supplied. The download remains a release-consistent, **non-atomic** registry export.

**Important:** The previously archived March 2026 aggregate figures, manuscripts and graphs have **not** been regenerated by this code change. Do not reuse their rates as if computed with this corrected denominator. A fresh complete source export, derived parquet and independent verification are required. These flags do **not** establish FDAAA 801 applicability, legal deadlines or misconduct.
